Miru can DM you when a new episode of something on your **Watching** or **Rewatching** list airs. Episode alerts are part of **[Miru+](/premium/)**.

## Turning them on

<Steps>

1. [Link AniList](/docs/commands/link/) if you haven't already.

2. Make sure you share a server with Miru and have **Allow direct messages from server members** turned on for it.

3. Run [`/alerts`](/docs/commands/alerts/) and press **Alerts: on**. Miru sends a test DM to check it can reach you.

</Steps>

`/alerts` also shows the next episodes coming up, and lets you choose [when alerts arrive](/docs/commands/alerts/#when-alerts-arrive): a delay, quiet hours or a daily digest.

## The alert DM

Each DM says **New episode out now** (or **Final episode out now**) and lets you know if you're behind. It has three buttons:

- **Mark episode N watched**: logs it on AniList.
- **Mute this show**: stops alerts for that show. Unmute from `/alerts`.
- **Watch on &lt;site&gt;**: the first legal stream AniList knows of.

These buttons keep working even after Miru restarts.

## Timing

Miru checks for new episodes every 5 minutes, so an alert can arrive up to about 5 minutes after the episode airs. If Miru was offline for a while, it catches up on episodes from up to 3 hours back.

<Aside type="caution" title="Private entries">
Entries marked **private** on AniList don't trigger alerts.
</Aside>

## Not getting alerts?

- Run `/alerts` to check alerts are on, and that your Miru+ is active.
- Check you share a server with Miru and allow DMs from its members.
- Check the show is on **Watching** or **Rewatching**, not Planning or Paused.
- Check the entry isn't private on AniList.
- Check you haven't muted the show.
