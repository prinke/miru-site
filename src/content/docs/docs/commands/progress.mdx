```
/progress <title> [episode] [score]
```

Log progress or rate something on your AniList list. Needs a [linked account](/docs/commands/link/). The reply is private.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `title` | Autocompletes from your **Watching**, **Rewatching**, **Paused** and **Planning** lists (anime and manga together). Each suggestion shows where you are, like `Ep 3/12` or `Ch 40/?`. |
| `episode` | Set progress to an exact number. You can go backwards. |
| `score` | Score out of 10. Decimals are fine (`8.5`), and `0` clears the score. |

## Examples

| You type | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `/progress title:Frieren` | Adds **+1** episode |
| `/progress title:Frieren episode:12` | Sets progress to episode 12 |
| `/progress title:Frieren score:9` | Sets your score to 9 and leaves progress alone |
| `/progress title:Frieren episode:28 score:9.5` | Sets progress and score together |

## Finishing a show

When you log the last episode or chapter, Miru marks it **Completed** 🎉 and suggests rating it.

## Binge button

The reply has a **+1 episode** (or **+1 chapter**) button, so you can keep logging without retyping the command. It lasts **5 minutes**.

<Aside type="tip" title="Not on your list?">
`/progress` only works for titles already on your list. Add it first with the **Add to list** button on [`/anime`](/docs/commands/anime/) or [`/manga`](/docs/commands/manga/).
</Aside>
