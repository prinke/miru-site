```
/season [season] [year] [sort] [format]
```

Browse a season's anime chart. No AniList account needed. The reply is public.

## Options

| Option | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `season` | Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall | Current season |
| `year` | 1940 or later | Current year |
| `sort` | Popularity, Score, Trending, Start date | Popularity |
| `format` | TV, Movies, ONA, OVA & specials | Everything |

## The chart

The chart shows 10 shows per page, with **Previous** and **Next** buttons. Each line shows the format, score, episode count and a countdown to the next episode.

If you've [linked AniList](/docs/commands/link/), each show is tagged with your list status, like `Watching` or `Planning`.

Pick a show from the **Open a show…** dropdown to see its full card, with [list buttons](/docs/features/list-buttons/) if you're linked. **Back to chart** takes you back.

## Seasons

Seasons follow AniList:

| Season | Months |
| --- | --- |
| Winter | December – February |
| Spring | March – May |
| Summer | June – August |
| Fall | September – November |

<Aside type="caution" title="December counts as next year">
December belongs to the *next* year's Winter. Shows starting in December 2026 are in Winter 2027.
</Aside>

## Good to know

- Controls last **10 minutes**.
