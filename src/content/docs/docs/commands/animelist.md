```
/animelist [status]
/mangalist [status]
```

Browse your AniList list from Discord. Needs a [linked account](/docs/commands/link/).

## Options

`status` is any [list status](/docs/features/list-buttons/#list-statuses). It defaults to **Watching** for anime and **Reading** for manga.

## The list

Each page shows 10 entries. Every entry shows:

- Your progress as `progress/total` (`?` when the total isn't known yet)
- The title, linking to AniList
- Your score

Use the **Change list** dropdown to switch status. **Previous** and **Next** buttons appear when there's more than one page.

## Good to know

- The reply is **public**, so everyone in the channel can see your list.
- Controls last **5 minutes**.
