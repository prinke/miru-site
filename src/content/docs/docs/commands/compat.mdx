```
/compat [user] [username] [type]
```

Compare your taste with someone else's. Needs a [linked account](/docs/commands/link/). The reply is public.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `user` | A Discord member who has linked AniList |
| `username` | Any AniList username |
| `type` | **Anime** (default) or **Manga** |

Give either `user` or `username`.

## The result

- A compatibility percentage, with a bar and a verdict
- Genres you share
- **You both loved**: titles you both rated highly
- **Where you disagree**: titles you rated very differently
- **X should try**: picks for each of you from the other's favourites, flagged if they're already on that person's Planning list

| Score | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| 85% and up | *Taste twins* |
| 70% and up | *Great match* |
| 55% and up | *Good match* |
| 40% and up | *Some common ground* |
| Below 40% | *Opposites attract?* |

## How it's calculated

The percentage blends three things:

- **Score agreement** on titles you've both scored. This needs at least 5 titles you've both scored.
- **Genre similarity** across your lists.
- **Overlap** between your lists.

<Aside>
Because the reply is posted in the channel, `/compat` only uses public AniList data. Private entries are left out, and private profiles can't be compared.
</Aside>
