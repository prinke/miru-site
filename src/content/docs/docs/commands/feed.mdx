The feed posts members' AniList activity in a channel. For what the posts look like and how it works, see [Server feed](/docs/features/server-feed/).

`/feed` opens a settings panel that only you can see. It shows whether the feed is on, its channel and mode, how many members have joined, and whether you have.

<Aside type="note" title="Server install only">
`/feed` only exists when Miru is [installed to the server](/docs/getting-started/#1-add-miru). It won't appear if you only added Miru as a personal app.
</Aside>

## Joining and leaving

Press **Join the feed** to share *your* AniList activity in this server, or **Leave the feed** to stop. Joining needs a [linked account](/docs/commands/link/).

Nobody is ever added automatically. Joining also includes you in [`/recommend for:This server`](/docs/commands/recommend/#for-this-server) and the server ranking on [`/wrapped`](/docs/commands/wrapped/#rankings).

## Settings for admins

Members with **Manage Server** also see:

- **A channel picker.** Choosing a text or announcement channel turns the feed on there, or moves it. Miru needs **View Channel**, **Send Messages** and **Embed Links** in that channel, and says so if it's missing any.
- **A mode menu**, once the feed is on:

  | Mode | Posts |
  | --- | --- |
  | **Highlights** (default) | Finishes, drops, rewatch and reread completions, and new starts |
  | **Everything** | Every episode and chapter |

- **Turn the feed off.** Members who joined stay joined, so turning it back on picks up where it left off.

Each change updates the panel straight away. The panel keeps working until you dismiss it.
