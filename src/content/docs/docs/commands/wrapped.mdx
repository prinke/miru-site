```
/wrapped [year]
```

Your year in anime and manga, as a PNG card you can share. Needs a [linked account](/docs/commands/link/). The reply is public.

## Options

`year` is 2010 or later. It defaults to the current year.

## What counts

A title counts towards a year if you **started or finished** it that year, based on the start and finish dates on your AniList entries.

## The card

- Estimated hours watched, marked "≈" because a show you started the year before counts in full
- Anime and manga counts
- Episodes and chapters
- Titles completed
- Mean score
- Top 3 genres and top studio
- Your busiest month
- Your favourite
- A "hot take": a title where your score differs from the average by 15 points or more
- A strip of covers
- Your AniList profile colour

## Rankings

The card can show where you rank:

- **Globally**: "Top X%", once 5 or more people have run Wrapped for that year.
- **In this server**: once 3 or more members who [joined the feed](/docs/commands/feed/#joining-and-leaving) have run Wrapped. Server install only.

Rankings use watch minutes plus 5 minutes per manga chapter.

<Aside>
Private entries count towards your totals, but they're never named on the card.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Empty card?">
Wrapped relies on start and finish dates. Entries edited outside AniList's website may not have them. You can add the dates on AniList and run `/wrapped` again.
</Aside>
